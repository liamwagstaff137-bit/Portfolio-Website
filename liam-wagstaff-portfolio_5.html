Sheet A-01 — About

About me

Good design starts with curiosity, and mine has always been restless. I'm in the final stages of a Bachelor of Architecture and Environments at the University of Sydney, but the real story of how I think started earlier — in a passion for multi-faceted visual design, storytelling, and building narrative through whatever medium the idea calls for. I'm drawn to creative thinking and problem-solving that takes initiative, and I go looking for the outside-the-box answer rather than waiting for it to arrive.

Travel feeds that curiosity directly. Every city I've walked through has taught me something a textbook can't: how a street reads at ground level, how materials weather in a different climate, how people actually use the spaces architects design for them. That instinct to seek out and study the built environment first-hand runs straight through everything I make.

It shows up in the work. I've built real knowledge across architectural design, urban planning, and sustainability within the built environment, backed by strong visual communication skills and fluency across multiple design software packages. I don't just use these tools — I use them to make ideas legible, to turn a concept into something a client or a community can actually see themselves in.

And it shows up in how I work with people. Roles in coaching, customer service, and team-based environments have sharpened my communication, collaboration, and problem-solving under pressure, while my time with the Landscaping and Nursery team at Bunnings Warehouse gave me a genuinely practical understanding of construction materials and the built environment industry from the ground up. I'm motivated by projects that combine innovation, sustainability, and clear communication — the kind of work that leaves a place, and the people in it, better off.

Outside of study, rugby, surfing and running keep me sharp — they're where I get my discipline, resilience and sense of teamwork, all of which follow me straight back into the studio.
